Five phases. One button between you and the runway.

Most sim tools do one job. ABRP covers the evening end to end, and every phase hands its data to the next.

01 · PLAN

Pick a flight that actually exists.

Search by fleet, region, airline and duration. Every route card carries the information a dispatcher would hand you.

  • Live METAR, D-ATIS and active runway on every card. Bad weather sorts to the top if you want a challenge.
  • One-click SimBrief with the flight number and the runway already filled in.
  • Fresh routes hides city pairs you flew recently. Group repeats folds 15,000 rows into the ones worth reading.
  • Trip Planner turns a pasted itinerary into legs, Free Route searches any pair at all.

02 · LAUNCH

One click. The sim starts set up for this flight.

Launch + Capture runs your whole pre-flight routine in order, then starts MSFS and quietly arms the recorder for your takeoff roll.

  • Only the scenery and aircraft this flight needs are linked into Community, so the sim loads lean.
  • Background apps close for the flight and come back when the sim does.
  • Your chosen graphics profile is written to the sim first, backed up and verified.
  • GSX profiles that ship with your sceneries are installed and kept up to date.

03 · FLY

Always know which frequency you should be on.

Live ATC reads your position, checks who is online on VATSIM, applies top-down coverage rules down to the sector, and tells you the frequency to tune, in a small overlay on top of the sim.

  • Next-up standby so the handoff is dialled before Center calls you.
  • Pre-flight frequency stack from your filed plan: Delivery to Ground at the far end.
  • Landing rating pops up after touchdown: fpm, G, crosswind, bounces.

04 · MEASURE

Every frame of every flight, on the record.

ABRP logs frame timing, video memory and CPU load for the whole flight, splits it into taxi, climb, cruise, descent and taxi-in, and files a report the moment you close the sim. This is a real flight.

  • Felt stutters, the hitches over 100 ms you actually notice, counted per hour.
  • Spike forensics names the cause: CPU, GPU, the display path, or another app.
  • Scenery impact ranks which payware airports cost you the most on the ground.

05 · IMPROVE

Your settings, decided by your own flights.

Sim Profiles keeps three setups and a dot that says which one flies next. Change one setting at a time, fly it three times, and ABRP judges it against your usual for the same aircraft and the same airports. Only changes that stay smooth and keep video-memory headroom make the cut.

  • 🔒 Baseline never changes. Fly it after a sim update to see whether the update hurt you.
  • ⭐ ABRP Suggested grows one proven change at a time.
  • 🧪 Experimental holds the one change under test. Tests can arrive from Claude by themselves.

The rest of the toolbox.

Everything a serious simmer usually juggles across a dozen utilities, folders and browser tabs.

Challenging & scenic approaches

Curated famous approaches with live METAR. Scenic mode only shows ones where today's wind favours the pretty runway.

My Airports

Scans your scenery folder, matches every pack to its ICAO, and links or unlinks it with a checkbox.

Aircraft & utilities

Keep add-ons outside Community and link only what tonight's flight uses. Liveries ride along.

Companion apps

vPilot, charts, SayIntentions and friends start with the sim and close with it if you want.

Compare & drift alerts

Group flights by sim version, GPU driver, aircraft or TLOD. A banner warns when an update made things worse.

AutoFPS aware

Draws AutoFPS's real TLOD line on every flight chart and keeps dynamic-TLOD flights out of fixed comparisons.

Maintenance

Shader-cache and aircraft WASM cleaners, NVIDIA profile backup, and Windows Defender exclusions for the sim.

Backups & updates

Export your whole setup in one zip, archive old captures, and pick up new versions silently on launch.
