MSFS Maintenance
Cache cleaners, NVIDIA settings backup, ABRP setup backup, and flight-log storage.
ABRP Setup Backup
Export everything that defines this install — all settings + your route registry + the 20,000-route snapshot — as one .zip. Import restores it on a fresh install or new PC (your current setup is backed up first, automatically). Flight logs are separate; archive them below.
Flight Log Storage
Raw frame captures are ~93% of your flight-log disk use, but all analysis (Baseline, Compare, reports) reads only the small summaries. Archiving compresses older raw captures in place (~79% smaller, fully reversible, nothing is ever deleted) and keeps the newest 5 uncompressed. CapFrameX export reads archived captures transparently.
Shader Cache Cleaner
Clears the MSFS / NVIDIA / DX12 / Steam shader caches (7 locations). Worth doing after a sim or GPU-driver update. Caches rebuild on their own — expect the next flight or two to be slightly stuttery while shaders recompile.
Before: disable Shader Cache in the NVIDIA App → reboot → close MSFS & overlays. After: re-enable NVIDIA Shader Cache → reboot again. Two reboots — the first one releases the locked cache so it can be deleted. (Full steps are in the confirm dialog.)
Aircraft WASM Cache (PMDG / Fenix)
Rebuilds an aircraft’s compiled WASM modules fresh. When: after a major Sim Update, or after a PMDG/Fenix update if you hit crashes / the “orange screen”. Not needed for GPU-driver updates. Your saved panel states & options (the “work” folder) are preserved.
NVIDIA Control Panel Settings (Backup / Restore)
Saves & restores your NVIDIA global 3D settings + game profiles (incl. MSFS) — the two driver .bin files. Back up while your settings are dialed in (e.g. before a driver wipe / DDU); Restore after a driver reinstall, then reboot. Previous backups are kept, timestamped.
